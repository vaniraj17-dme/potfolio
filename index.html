Fusion — The Luxury Salon: 2-Week Reel Calendar

Built around what's already working on the account: implied-story framing, the male stylist as a recurring character, tease-and-reveal, and zero static graphics.

WEEK 1

Reel 1 — "The Silent Transformation" (Client Story)

Format: Client-story, cinematic Hook (0–2s): Close-up on client's hands, fidgeting in her lap. No face yet. Text overlay: "She hadn't cut her hair in 3 years." Shot list:

  1. Hands / lap / nervous energy (2s)
  2. Stylist's hands sectioning hair, no dialogue (3–4s)
  3. Quick cuts of scissors, no face reveal — build tension (4–5s)
  4. Slow reveal: mirror shot, her face seeing it first (payoff, 3s)
  5. Genuine reaction — unscripted, hold the shot Caption: "3 years of the same hair. One decision to change it. Tag someone who needs this push. 💇‍♀️" Audio: Trending emotional/build audio, not talking

Reel 2 — "Character Intro: The Stylist" (Recurring Character)

Format: Mini-doc, establishes the male stylist as a face of the brand Hook: He looks directly at camera, mid-task, doesn't stop working. Text: "Ask him why he only does 4 clients a day." Shot list:

  1. Working shot, in motion, tattoos/rings visible (his existing visual signature)
  2. Voiceover or text: his philosophy in one line
  3. Cut to a finished client walking out, backs turned (reuse the tease framing that hit 7k) Caption: "Not everyone gets an appointment. Here's why. #FusionTheLuxurySalon" Note: This is the seed reel for a recurring series — plan to reference "him" in future captions

Reel 3 — "Renovation Tease Pt. 2" (Curiosity)

Format: Behind-the-scenes tease, continuing the 5.8k-view renovation thread Hook: Same construction space, visibly further along. Text: "3 weeks left." Shot list:

  1. Walk-through shot, unfinished space
  2. Stylist gesturing to a specific corner/detail (chair, mirror, light)
  3. End on a closed door / covered mirror — withhold the reveal Caption: "Not ready yet. But close. 👀" Note: Part of a 3-part arc — do NOT reveal the space until Reel 8

Reel 4 — "Bridal Macro Moment" (Replicate the 54.6k winner)

Format: Emotional/aspirational, no wide shots Hook: Extreme close-up — jewellery, hands adjusting a dupatta, or a single tear/nervous breath Shot list:

  1. Macro on jewellery/hands (2–3s)
  2. Slow pan to eyes only, not full face (2s)
  3. Cut to groom or family waiting — implied context, never shown fully
  4. Final full shot only in the last frame Caption: "Some moments aren't meant to be rushed. #BridalByFusion" Note: This format is the account's proven top performer — replicate the style, not the same bride

WEEK 2

Reel 5 — "The Decision Point" (Client Story #2)

Format: Story with a real dilemma Hook: Client mid-conversation with stylist, looking unsure. Text: "She almost said no." Shot list:

  1. Consultation moment — stylist explaining, client hesitant
  2. Quick montage of the process (fast cuts, no lingering)
  3. Reveal + reaction, held longer than the process shots Caption: "The best transformations start with almost saying no. Would you have said yes?"

Reel 6 — "Myth-Bust: Stylist POV" (Authority/Save-worthy)

Format: Talking-to-camera or text-led, stylist as expert Hook: Text: "3 things we check before touching your hair color — most salons skip #2." Shot list:

  1. Stylist examining hair strand under light
  2. Text overlay listing the 3 points, one at a time, with matching visual
  3. End on a finished color result Caption: "Save this before your next appointment. 📌" Note: This is your save/share-driver — make the info genuinely useful, not generic

Reel 7 — "Retro Couple, New Story" (Trend + Aesthetic reuse)

Format: Styled aesthetic shoot, same visual language as the retro Bollywood-style post (2,380 views) but with narrative text Hook: Text overlay establishing a mini scene: "First look. Same salon, 10 years later." Shot list:

  1. Styled portrait shots, film-still framing
  2. Cut to a "before" style throwback if available (real client anniversary angle is even stronger) Caption: "Some love stories start at Fusion. Literally."

Reel 8 — "The Reveal" (Payoff to Renovation Arc)

Format: Big reveal reel — closes the 3-week tease Hook: Text: "3 weeks. Here it is." Shot list:

  1. Same covered-mirror shot from Reel 3, now uncovered
  2. Full walkthrough, music swell
  3. End on the team standing in the finished space Caption: "This is what we've been building. Come see it. 🥂" Note: Push this one with a small boost/ad spend — it's the culmination of a 2-week story arc and should outperform standalone posts

Cadence & Rules Going Forward

  • 4 reels/week, mixing: 1 client-story, 1 stylist-authority, 1 tease/arc, 1 aesthetic/trend
  • No static graphic posts — convert any promo card into a moving reel with text-on-video
  • Every reel ends on an unscripted reaction shot, held for at least 1–2 seconds before cutting
  • Captions always end with a tag-prompt or a question — this is what converts views into shares
  • Track which format each week's top performer belongs to and shift the next week's mix toward it
