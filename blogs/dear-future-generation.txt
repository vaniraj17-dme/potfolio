# Dear future generation... Hello!

Dear future generation,

It is day four hundred fifty-six – or something of that effect – of the ongoing pandemic. I have yet to get out of bed as I write this letter to you. It is the year 2021, almost one year since the world went into a complete lockdown. While I had prepared myself for a zombie apocalypse, I don’t know what to do when I am just sitting at home, bored, and there is no tangible enemy to fight, and no, the boss doesn’t count.

So, I am sitting in my bed; I have an office meeting in half an hour AT 8:30 IN THE MORNING!!! Who makes their employees attend a meeting at such an ungodly hour? But, well, it is just one of the perks of working from home… There are no time limits or office hours to restrict the constant flow of office calls and work. We are expected to be available 24*7 and keep working – I have forgotten what a Sunday looks like now. So, my first lesson to you, dear future generation, is if someone offers permanent or extended work from home, scream NO. If that doesn’t help, scratch, claw, and run in the opposite direction.

And if you have neighbors who have kids, move out; those things are *whispers* ‘monsters’. I can spy on my sweet neighbor from my bedroom window. Well, she used to be sweet, at least until last year. Now she just appears picky and disgruntled most of the time. Not that we talk as much, you know, pandemic and all that, but because I can see her losing her cool every day. She has also started picking at her hair, which is a very weird habit to begin with. And here is a bit of gossip; I heard she lost her job too. No, not because of the pandemic but because she was getting too loud and bossy and, as I told you, disgruntled. And on top of that, she asked for a raise from her employers – imagine the audacity of it, phew! And then there are those kids of hers; I can constantly hear them shouting loudly and, maybe, playing or fighting, whatever it is that they are doing. And they are constantly hanging on to their mother, one sure way to get spoiled.

One of my other neighbors organized a secret party recently, and that has been one of the most exciting things that happened in the past year. We, around 15-20 of us, gathered on the roof of her house and partied the night away. We didn’t have to wear masks, which was such a relief. I honestly can’t breathe in those things. Whoever suggested that the government introduce a mask mandate should be punished thoroughly. I mean, if it was a fashion statement, I would have understood, but this… senseless. What are masks supposed to do? Are they really going to protect us? If they could, so many people wouldn’t have contacted COVID-19 (given that it actually happened). I think the whole pandemic is an elaborate scheme by the government to control us. If COVID-19 was a real threat and people are dying in enormous numbers because of it, then why aren’t any prominent leaders dead? Have they got a special immunity, or is it just the immunity of lies and a global façade that is protecting them?

And then there is the drive to get vaccinated. I, for one, don’t really believe in the pandemic and don’t intend to get vaccinated. A lot of people are getting sick after receiving vaccines, and I don’t want to be one on the list- the vaccines are supposed to be safe, are they not? One of my friends developed a fever after getting vaccinated; I scolded her for taking such a drastic step. After all, the pandemic isn’t real. Even if it was, why would you put your body through such a thing! Anyway, she and I aren’t friends anymore. I don’t want anyone in my life who is so beyond reason and wants to kill my vibe. Do you know she even tried to defend the fever? She said that it is normal to develop a fever or a sore throat after a dose of vaccination since a little bit of virus is introduced into your body, thus helping it develop antibodies against it and recognize the virus as a threat in the future and fight against it. But if you are introducing my body to a dose of the virus, aren’t you potentially putting me at risk? Sometimes, I don’t understand people and how blinded they can be. I have vowed not to get vaccinated, and neither should you if they have such side effects. Besides, covid is supposed to be like a regular flu, so it’s all good.

Anyway, I think I have rambled on enough, and I will wrap up my letter now. But every time you read a COVID-19-related story, remember that it is government-made propaganda; it is not real – never was. But we live in silent times; there are only a few raising their voice in dissent while most others choose to live with their heads down. I have got nothing against them either, it is their choice, but I won’t be quietened. I will sit at my home, disheveled and in my pajamas, waiting for the office meeting to start and telling people what a façade of a pandemic we live in. I will call my friends and complain and complain till my voice is sore and my throat hurts, but I won’t keep quiet. I cannot take to the streets like other protestors; I have a lot of work to do – office, cooking, cleaning, whatnot – but that doesn’t mean I am not supporting them in spirit.

NOTE: The writer is being sarcastic – all the things mentioned in the article are said in good humor.
